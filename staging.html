🩸 CBC Red Cell Indices (रक्त कण आकार)

Hemoglobin (Hb), RBC aur PCV (Hematocrit) dalte hi cells ka size (MCV), weight (MCH) aur concentration (MCHC) apne aap nikal aate hain:

• MCV = (PCV × 10) ÷ RBC (Normal: 80 - 100 fL)
• MCH = (Hb × 10) ÷ RBC (Normal: 27 - 32 pg)
• MCHC = (Hb × 100) ÷ PCV (Normal: 32 - 36 g/dL)

🥗 Lipid Friedewald Formula (हृदय व कोलेस्ट्रॉल)

Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides aur HDL se VLDL aur LDL (Harmful cholesterol) khud nikal jaata hai:

• VLDL = Triglycerides ÷ 5
• LDL = Total Cholesterol - HDL - VLDL
• Chol / HDL Ratio = Total Cholesterol ÷ HDL
