Lab Math & Formulas

LIS Engine
🩸 CBC Red Cell Indices

Calculates MCV, MCH, and MCHC based on RBC Count, Hemoglobin, and Hematocrit (PCV).

MCV = (PCV × 10) / RBC
MCH = (Hb × 10) / RBC
MCHC = (Hb × 100) / PCV
🥗 Lipid Friedewald Formula

Derives VLDL, LDL, and Chol/HDL ratio automatically from Triglycerides and Total Cholesterol.

VLDL = Triglycerides / 5
LDL = Total Chol - HDL - VLDL
Ratio = Total Chol / HDL
🧪 LFT & KFT Ratios
Globulin = Total Protein - Albumin
A/G Ratio = Albumin / Globulin
BUN = Blood Urea / 2.14
